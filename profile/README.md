# MIRA Lab — Machine Intelligence for Robot Autonomy

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the **MIRA Lab** at the **Italian Institute of Artificial Intelligence for Industry (AI4I)**, hosted at Officine Grandi Riparazioni (OGR) in Turin, Italy.

Our goal is to develop the algorithmic foundations and system-level methodologies that enable AI-powered autonomous systems to operate safely, efficiently, and reliably in high-stakes, real-world environments. We combine **AI / Machine Learning**, **Control Theory**, and **Mathematical Optimization** across aerospace robotics, future mobility systems, and autonomy at large.

🌐 **Website:** [mira.rd-labs.ai4i.it](https://mira.rd-labs.ai4i.it/)

---

### Research Thrusts

* **Generalist Robot Policies:** Physical AI tools and real-time learning/model-based algorithms that generalize across tasks, environments, and robot platforms.
* **Coordinated Autonomy:** Learning and optimization methods for multi-agent decision-making across vehicle fleets, spacecraft constellations, and human-robot teams.
* **Physical AI Safety:** Runtime monitoring, guardrailing of learned policies, and reasoning-based mechanisms to anticipate hazards and synthesize recovery behavior on the fly.
* **Societally-Impactful Autonomy:** Pushing autonomy to its limits in safety-critical domains such as space robotics and autonomous mobility.
